A bookkeeping firm built around the phone call

Hollis & Reed started because two bookkeepers kept hearing the same complaint: nobody at the old firm ever picked up.

How it started

Marian Hollis and Tom Reed met at a mid-sized accountancy practice where small clients were, in the firm's own words, a rounding error. Questions waited days for an answer. Year-end was a scramble because nobody had looked at the books since the last one.

In 2016 they left and took eleven clients with them, with one promise: every client gets a named bookkeeper who answers the phone, and the books are closed every month whether or not anything is due.

Nine years later the firm looks after 140 businesses across the South West. The promise has not changed. Neither has the phone number.

What we hold ourselves to

Three rules, written into every engagement letter.

Reply the same day

Messages sent before 15:00 get an answer the same working day. If the answer takes longer, you get told that the same day too.

Close every month

Reconciled, reviewed and summarised by the 10th. If we miss it, that month is free.

Say the uncomfortable thing

If the numbers show a problem, you hear it from us first, in plain words, with a suggestion.
