All services

Each one is priced on its own. Most clients combine two or three.

Monthly bookkeeping

Bank and card feeds reconciled weekly, receipts matched through your phone, and the month closed by the 10th. Includes a plain-language summary and a list of anything we need from you.

Payroll and pensions

Payslips sent on the same day each month, auto-enrolment handled, and HMRC submissions filed on time. Starters, leavers and changes go through a message to your bookkeeper.

VAT returns

Quarterly returns prepared from already-reconciled books and submitted under Making Tax Digital. We show the amount building up each month so the quarter is never a surprise.

Year-end accounts

Statutory accounts and corporation tax return, prepared from twelve closed months. Filed early, with a short note explaining what changed since last year and why.

Advisory

A quarterly call, or a monthly one if you prefer, to look at margins, cash runway and the cost of your next decision. No slide decks, just your numbers and a second opinion.

Catch-up bookkeeping

Behind by a few months or a few years? We rebuild the ledger from bank statements, fixed-price per month of backlog, and bring you to a clean starting point.

Questions people ask before switching

Do I have to change accounting software?

No. We work in Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent. If you are on spreadsheets or paper, we will recommend one and set it up, and the software subscription stays in your name.

What do you need from me each month?

Usually ten minutes: photograph receipts in the app and answer two or three questions about transactions we cannot identify. Everything else comes through the bank feed.

Can you work with my existing accountant?

Yes. Many clients keep their accountant for year-end and tax advice and use us for the monthly work. We hand over closed books, which usually lowers their fee.

How quickly can you start?

Setup takes two to three weeks from the first call. If you are mid-year, we start from the beginning of the current financial year so there is one clean set of books.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us how the books are kept today and we will suggest the smallest change that helps.
