Billing questions

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans run month to month with 30 days' notice. Annual plans are paid up front and include two months free; if you leave early we refund the unused months minus the discount.

What happens if I go over the transaction limit?

Nothing in the month it happens. If it happens two months running we suggest the next plan up and move you at the start of the following month.

Is the software included?

No. Your accounting software subscription stays in your name so you own your data. We will tell you which tier you need; it is usually the cheapest one.
