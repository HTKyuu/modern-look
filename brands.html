Adding a third company

Copy one of the two objects in config/brands.js, give it a new key, and change its values. Then point brand in config/site.config.js at the new key. The whole site, including this page, picks it up.

Run node scripts/check-contrast.mjs after changing colours. It checks every text and control pair in both themes against WCAG 2.2 AA and fails if one is short.

The full list of inputs, files and rules is in docs/SKILL-CONVERSION.md.
